Reference: Linux v7.0 MIT-licensed
[intel_ggtt.c](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v7.0/drivers/gpu/drm/i915/gt/intel_ggtt.c),
`gen6_gttmmadr_size()` and `gen6_gttadr_offset()`.

All 14 host checks passed with GCC C++17, `-Wall -Wextra -Werror`, no exceptions
or RTTI, and address/undefined-behavior sanitizers. They include the exact 8 MiB
offset, full 8 MiB table extent, 64-bit boundary and malformed resource cases.
Native compilation remains pending the next shared VM window. The component
hashes above predate patch 3 and must not be attributed to it.

This is not hardware readiness: inherited 32-bit PTE stores and other Gen12
memory/initialization paths remain unresolved. No performance claim is possible.
